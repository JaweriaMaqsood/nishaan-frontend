Rehnuma kya hai?

Rehnuma Pakistani students ke liye ek AI tutor website hai. Isay Javeria Maqsood ne banaya hai. Ye Class 1 se Entry Test tak sawal samjhata, MCQs banata aur kamzor topics batata hai.

Kya ye Urdu mein jawab deta hai?

Ji. Aap English, Urdu (Nastaliq) ya Roman Urdu chun sakti hain, aur poori website aur jawab usi zubaan mein aate hain.

Kaunse subjects hain?

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, English, Urdu, Computer Science, Islamiat aur Pakistan Studies, har subject ka alag tutor, aur MDCAT/ECAT ki tayyari.

Rehnuma kitne ka hai?

Pehli dafa use karne par 3 din poora free milte hain. Us ke baad roz 3 free sawal milte hain, aur unlimited use ke liye Premium plan hai (1 mahina, 3 mahine ya 1 saal), jis ki payment JazzCash se hoti hai.

Kya Rehnuma mere ustad ki jagah le sakta hai?

Nahi. Ye parhai mein madad deta hai: sawal samjhata hai aur practice karwata hai. School ya academy ka ustad apni jagah zaroori hai.
